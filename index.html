New vs Old Regime - Which is Better for You?

New Regime is better if: Income up to 12L (zero tax), you don't have many deductions, you want simple filing, you are salaried with no home loan. Old Regime is better if: You have home loan interest >1.5L, HRA exemption >1L, 80C full 1.5L + 80D 50k + other deductions total >3L, income >16L with heavy investments. Q1. What is zero tax limit in new regime 2026?
In new regime 2026, income up to Rs 12 Lakh taxable income is tax-free due to rebate u/s 87A. Gross up to ~Rs 12.75L with standard deduction is tax-free.
Q2. Which regime is better for 12 LPA?
New regime is better for 12 LPA - zero tax. Old regime would have ~Rs 1.2L tax without deductions, ~Rs 60k with full deductions. So new saves more.
